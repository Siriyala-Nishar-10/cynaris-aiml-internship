BM25 is a keyword ranking function used by classic search engines. It scores a document by looking at how often each query term appears in it, which is called term frequency, and how rare that term is across the whole collection, which is called inverse document frequency. Rare terms count for more than common ones. BM25 adds term frequency saturation, so repeating a word many times gives diminishing returns, and length normalisation, so long documents are not favoured just for containing more words. Two tuning parameters, usually called k1 and b, control saturation and length normalisation. BM25 needs no training and no embeddings, runs fast on a small server, and is very strong when the question uses the same words as the answer, such as product names, error codes or exact figures.
