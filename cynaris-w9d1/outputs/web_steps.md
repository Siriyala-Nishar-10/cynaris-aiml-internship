Running AI Assistants Locally with Ollama: Benefits and Limits for Small Business

As a small business owner, you may have considered leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI) to streamline operations, improve efficiency, and gain a competitive edge. One potential solution is running AI assistants locally with Ollama, an open-source software platform developed by Jeffrey Morgan and Michael Chiang. This briefing will explore the benefits and limitations of using Ollama for small businesses.

**Benefits of Running AI Assistants Locally with Ollama**

One of the key benefits of using Ollama is its ability to provide cost-free iteration for AI models. According to Allan Roberto's article "Running LLMs Locally with Ollama: Benefits, Limitations, and Use Cases", Ollama allows for offline development, which means you can experiment and refine your AI models without incurring cloud costs. This can be particularly beneficial for small businesses that want to test new AI-powered tools without breaking the bank.

Another advantage of Ollama is its ability to be used for privacy-sensitive work. With Ollama, you can run open-weight language models locally, which means you can maintain control over sensitive data and avoid cloud storage costs. This is a significant benefit for small businesses that handle sensitive customer information.

Ollama also offers a range of use cases, including building local-first AI apps and coding assistants. According to Anonark's article "Building an Offline AI Assistant with Ollama: Complete 2026 Guide", Ollama can be used to automate coding tasks, document analysis, and other tasks with open models. This can help small businesses streamline processes and improve productivity.

**Limitations of Running AI Assistants Locally with Ollama**

While Ollama offers several benefits, there are also some limitations to consider. One potential limitation is the price of Ollama's Pro and Teams plans. According to Ollama's pricing page, the Pro plan costs $20 per month, and the Teams plan costs $125 per month. While Ollama's free local option is still available, these costs can add up quickly, especially for small businesses with limited budgets.

Another limitation is the requirement for a Pro or Teams subscription to access some of Ollama's features. According to Ollama's pricing page, the Cloud pricing plans start at $20 per month for Pro and $125 per month for Teams. This can limit accessibility for small businesses that are on a tight budget or don't require these features.

**Conclusion**

Running AI assistants locally with Ollama can be a cost-effective and efficient solution for small businesses. With its ability to provide cost-free iteration, allow for privacy-sensitive work, and offer a range of use cases, Ollama is an attractive option for businesses looking to leverage AI. However, small businesses should be aware of Ollama's pricing limitations and consider whether the benefits outweigh the costs.
