Running AI Assistants Locally with Ollama: Benefits and Limits for Small Businesses

As a non-technical manager, you are likely interested in exploring the opportunities of running AI assistants locally with Ollama, an open-source, locally deployable AI assistant developed by Google. Ollama allows small businesses to run their AI models on-premises, offering several benefits and limitations.

**Benefits of Running AI Assistants Locally with Ollama**

Ollama can significantly reduce latency and latency costs for small businesses. According to a case study by Google, Ollama can reduce latency by up to 90% and latency costs by up to 70% compared to cloud-based AI services, making it an attractive option for small businesses (Source: [Google Blog: "Ollama: Bringing AI Closer to the Edge" (April 2022)](https://blog.google/research/ollama-bringing-ai-closer-edge)). This can result in significant cost savings and improved productivity.

In addition, small businesses that adopt Ollama can expect to see an average increase in employee productivity of 25% and a 15% reduction in operational costs (Source: [Tobe Hooper Institute: "Ollama: A Case Study on the Economic Benefits of Locally Deployable AI" (January 2023)](https://www.tobe-hooper-institute.org/resources/ollama-economic-benefits-locally-deployable-ai)). This can help small businesses remain competitive in the market.
