Hybrid search runs a keyword retriever and a dense retriever on the same question and merges the two ranked lists. A common merging method is reciprocal rank fusion, which gives each passage a score based on its rank in every list, so a passage that both retrievers rank highly rises to the top. The merged list is more robust than either retriever alone because the two methods fail on different questions: keyword search misses paraphrases, while dense search can miss exact terms. The price is extra latency and complexity, since two retrievers and a joiner must be maintained. Teams should measure on their own questions, because on small collections a single retriever can match or beat the hybrid.
